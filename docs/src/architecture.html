Sanjeevani Twin · System Architecture

A hybrid physiology + AI digital twin for Type 2 Diabetes in India. It fuses a static EHR with dynamic wearable streams, predicts glucose spikes 2 h ahead, explains them and simulates interventions.
1

Data sources

Static · EHR (FHIR R4)Demographics, diagnoses (SNOMED-CT), labs (LOINC: HbA1c, FPG, lipids, eGFR), meds (RxNorm), TCF7L2 genotype, polygenic risk, family history. Masked ABHA ID
Dynamic · wearablesCGM every 5 min · heart rate · HRV (RMSSD) · sleep stages · steps (Apple Health / Google Fit-like)
Patient logsMeals from a 28-item Indian food library (GI-aware), medication doses
Synthetic cohort generator40 Indian "virtual humans" × 14 days. Regional diets, Navratri fasting, late dinners, stress, sensor lag/noise/dropouts. Synthea-compatible

Digital twin core

EHR → Bayesian priorsBMI, waist (South-Asian weighting), age → SI; duration + TCF7L2 → β-cell γ; FPG → Gb; sulfonylurea → threshold
Physiology engineExtended Bergman minimal model: dual-speed gut absorption, exercise memory, circadian and sleep-debt modulation, stress-driven EGP, metformin effect
dG/dt = −(SG+X)G + SGGb′ + Ra − kexAG + …
Calibration (onboarding, 3 days)MAP cross-entropy search, 384 candidates × 3 rounds, batched NumPy, ~1 s/patient
Live assimilation + adaptive twinLag-compensated CGM observer every 5 min; nightly Bayesian re-calibration of SI and hepatic set-point; learned dawn phenomenon
4

Hybrid AI layer

Habit-aware twin forecastssimulate 120 min ahead, with and without the expected meal
Causal features (52)twin state + forecasts + its own recent errors, CGM trend, meals, steps, HRV/stress, sleep, circadian, EHR. Leak-tested
Residual quantile GBMslearn (CGM − twin) at 30/60/120 min · P10/P50/P90
Conformal calibrationCQR on disjoint calibration patients → honest 80% intervals
Spike classifier + isotonic calibrationP(new excursion above 180 within 2 h); alert threshold = max sensitivity at ≥75% precision

Why hybrid beats black-box ML

  • 2 training patients: RMSE 31.3 vs 38.1 (ML-only), 18% better
  • 26% fewer false alarms (0.65 vs 0.88/day) at higher precision (82% vs 79%)
  • Explains and simulates. A plain predictor cannot

Privacy & standards

  • Synthetic data only. DPDP Act 2023 / HIPAA-safe sandbox
  • FHIR R4, LOINC, SNOMED-CT, RxNorm
  • ABHA / ABDM-ready identifiers

Deployability

  • CPU-only, runs on a laptop; no GPU or cloud
  • Full pipeline in ~5 min; inference < 50 ms per patient
  • Dependencies: numpy, scipy, pandas, scikit-learn
